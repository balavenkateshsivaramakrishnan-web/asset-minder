# Asset Minder

Problem Statement — AMC Reminder App

Households own multiple appliances, vehicles, and equipment such as air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, RO systems, cars, bikes, inverters, and other home appliances. Each of these may have different service schedules, warranty periods, AMC contracts, insurance dates, and maintenance requirements.

Currently, people often manage these dates through paper bills, WhatsApp messages, calendars, memory, or scattered documents. As a result, they may:

Forget scheduled maintenance and servicing.

Miss AMC and warranty expiry dates.

Lose service invoices and warranty documents.

Spend more on repairs because routine maintenance was missed.

Have difficulty tracking how much they spend on each appliance.

Forget important vehicle-related renewal and service dates.

Have no single place to manage the maintenance of their household assets.

Proposed Solution

The proposed AMC Reminder App will provide a centralized platform where users can register household assets, store AMC and warranty details, maintain service history, upload documents, track expenses, and receive timely notifications before important dates.

The app will act as a personal household maintenance assistant, helping users keep their appliances and vehicles properly maintained while reducing the chances of missed services, expired contracts, and lost records.
